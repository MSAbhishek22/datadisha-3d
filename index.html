Every state and union territory as a block on a table. The height of a block is one real number, its colour is another. Turn the table, click a block, put two states side by side — the census stops being a spreadsheet and starts being a place.

Chapter one

The problem: a census nobody can see

In 2011, India counted everyone. The result is one of the largest public datasets on earth — and almost nobody ever looks at it, because it lives in tables with hundreds of rows. A table can tell you that one state's literacy rate is 94% and another's is 62%. It cannot make you feel the distance between those two numbers. This page puts the distance on a table, in 3D, where your eyes can measure it.
